## Final Boss

Everything you have learned, combined. The summit is guarded by a **clearance token unique to your team**, exported to `clearance.dat`.

The export was wrapped in several layers so it would survive a trip through a mail gateway. Peel them off, in the right order, to recover your team's token. It is your flag.

> The file is **unique to your team**. Submitting another team's token is detected and reported to the organisers.

**Tools that help:** `base64 -d`, a hex decoder, and a loop over 255 XOR keys. CyberChef does all three.
